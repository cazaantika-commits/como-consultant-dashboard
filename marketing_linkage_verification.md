# Marketing Page Linkage Verification

Browser verification project: **مجان متعدد الاستخدامات (G+4P+25)**. The project is loaded in the Financial Studies module for a direct comparison of the saved Marketing page distribution with the marketing expense rows in the cash-flow reports.

The saved Marketing page shows a total AED 16,091,064 budget distributed equally as AED 487,608 in each project month from month 6 through month 22. The Investor Cash Flow contains a dedicated `التسويق` debit row for comparison with this saved range.

Browser verification confirmed the Investor Cash Flow shows AED 488K per month across the same months 6–22, which is the displayed rounding of the Marketing page’s AED 487,608 monthly value. The Escrow Cash Flow does not show a marketing row because the shared engine classifies marketing as an investor-funded cost, not an escrow-funded cost. Therefore, the saved Marketing page output is linked to Investor Cash Flow; it is intentionally excluded from Escrow Cash Flow.
