## User-approved rules

1. The completed Financial Studies **Project Aggregation** page, titled **المحفظة الاستثمارية — صافي التدفقات النقدية المجمّعة**, is finished and must remain unchanged.
2. Add a separate reversed presentation named **المحفظة الاستثمارية — العرض الشهري للتدفقات**. It uses the same exact signed monthly Investor Cash Flow data, but shows months as rows and projects as columns.
3. Keep both project-first and month-first presentations because different audiences prefer different reading directions. Neither replaces the other.
4. The existing detailed **Capital Portfolio** keeps its current visual structure, every existing column, and the exact current column order. Approved additions belong above the table; no existing column may be removed, merged, or reordered.
5. The detailed Capital Portfolio must be fed by current Financial Studies outputs rather than old or hard-coded report values. Saved edits in the underlying Financial Studies pages must flow through to the report.
6. The detailed Capital Portfolio should show, at the top: total revenue, total project cost, profit before the developer share, and total investor capital split into paid and remaining. Projects classified as `build_for_rent` are excluded from revenue and profit metrics because they do not have sales revenue.
7. Use a stable colored marker/dot for each project in portfolio reports to make rows and columns easier to follow.
8. Every approved portfolio report must provide HTML and Excel export and end with a visible total row.
