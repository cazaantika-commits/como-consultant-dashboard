# Pre-Completion Commission Timing Verification

Browser verification project: **مجان متعدد الاستخدامات (G+4P+25)**.

The selected project and saved Sales Plan are loaded in the Financial Studies module. The following verification checks the escrow-funded sales-commission row against the payment schedule saved by the Sales Plan.

The saved Majan payment plan shows 10% on signing, 10% one month later, periodic installments, and 40% on handover. The first sale batch occurs in project month 7 for AED 15.7M. Its 10% signing receipt is AED 1.6M in month 7; the second 10% receipt brings total buyer receipts for that batch to 20% in month 8. The Escrow Cash Flow shows the matching 5% commission of AED 786K in month 8. The next sale batch is sold in month 8 and its AED 1.1M commission appears in month 9, again when that batch reaches the 20% buyer-receipts threshold.
